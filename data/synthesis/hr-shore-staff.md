# Synthesis prompt: hr-shore-staff

```text
Write three internal HR policy documents for a fictional Indian ship-management company,
"Konkan Marine Services Pvt. Ltd.", with offices in Kochi (head office) and Mumbai.
Audience: shore-based office staff (not seafarers).

Documents:
1. Leave Policy for Shore Staff
2. Travel and Expenses Policy
3. Code of Conduct and Grievance Procedure

For each document:
- Start with one line saying the company is fictional and the document is synthetic.
- 9 numbered sections ("## 1. Scope", "## 2. ..."), each 60 to 110 words.
- Every section must contain at least two concrete, checkable facts: numbers of days,
  amounts in INR or USD, deadlines, thresholds, or named approvers.
- Use realistic Indian corporate HR practice (for example 26 weeks maternity leave,
  an Internal Committee for harassment complaints) but invent all specific figures.
- Keep the three documents consistent with each other (for example the travel policy
  may refer to approvers named in the code of conduct).
- Do NOT include: salary bands, individual employees' leave balances, or any personal
  data. These are deliberately left out so the factory's groundability guard has a
  real gap to detect.

Output plain Markdown, one document at a time.
```
