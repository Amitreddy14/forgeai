# Travel and Expenses Policy

Konkan Marine Services Pvt. Ltd. (fictional company). Synthetic document for demonstration. Version 2.0, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all shore staff travelling on company business within India and abroad. Travel for vessel attendance by superintendents is covered by the Technical Department's procedures, but expense claims still follow sections 7 and 8 of this policy.

## 2. Approval

Every business trip needs a travel request approved by the reporting manager before booking. International trips also need approval from the department head. Trips that are not approved in advance are not reimbursed unless the department head confirms in writing that the trip was urgent.

## 3. Air travel

All flights must be booked through the corporate travel desk at least 7 days before departure. Economy class is standard for flights of up to 6 hours. Business class may be booked for flights longer than 6 hours with approval from a director. Changes to bookings made for personal reasons are paid by the employee.

## 4. Hotels

Hotel stays are capped at INR 7,500 per night in Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru, and INR 5,000 per night in other Indian cities. For international travel the cap is USD 180 per night. Stays above the cap need prior written approval from the travel desk. Employees staying with friends or family may claim INR 1,000 per night instead.

## 5. Daily allowance

The daily allowance for domestic travel is INR 1,800 per day and covers meals and incidental expenses. For international travel the daily allowance is USD 65 per day. If a meal is provided by the host or the hotel package, the daily allowance is reduced by 25 percent for each such meal.

## 6. Local transport

Taxis and app-based cabs are reimbursed at actual cost with receipts. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre and a personal two-wheeler at INR 6 per kilometre. Airport transfers in premium cab categories are not reimbursed unless travelling with clients.

## 7. Travel advances

Employees may request a travel advance of up to 75 percent of the estimated trip cost. Advances must be settled by submitting the expense claim within 15 days of returning. A new advance is not issued while a previous advance remains unsettled.

## 8. Expense claims

Expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of returning from the trip. Original receipts are required for any single expense above INR 500. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary run. Claims submitted after 60 days are paid only with approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## 9. Non-reimbursable expenses

The company does not reimburse alcohol, minibar charges, laundry for trips shorter than 4 nights, traffic fines, personal entertainment, or expenses for accompanying family members. Lost receipts may be replaced by a signed declaration no more than twice per calendar year.
