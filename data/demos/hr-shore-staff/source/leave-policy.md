# Leave Policy for Shore Staff

Konkan Marine Services Pvt. Ltd. (fictional company). Synthetic document for demonstration. Version 3.2, effective 1 April 2026.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers permanent and fixed-term employees working in the Kochi head office and the Mumbai branch office. It does not cover seafarers serving on company vessels, whose leave is governed by their articles of agreement and the applicable collective bargaining agreement. Interns and consultants are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Annual leave

Shore staff earn 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days for each completed month of service. Employees in their first year may use leave only after completing 3 months of service. Annual leave must be taken in blocks of at least half a day.

## 3. Carry forward and encashment

Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of that year or they lapse. On resignation or retirement, unused annual leave up to a maximum of 30 days is paid out at the employee's basic daily salary. Leave cannot be encashed while in service.

## 4. Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for any absence longer than 2 consecutive working days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward and is never encashed. Sick leave taken during annual leave converts to sick leave only with a medical certificate.

## 5. Maternity, paternity and adoption leave

Birth mothers receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for each of the first two children, and 12 weeks for any later child. Fathers and partners receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth. Employees who adopt a child under 3 months old receive 12 weeks of paid adoption leave.

## 6. Bereavement and compassionate leave

Employees receive 5 working days of paid bereavement leave on the death of a spouse, child, parent or sibling, and 2 working days for a grandparent or parent-in-law. Up to 3 additional days of compassionate leave may be granted by the Head of HR in exceptional circumstances.

## 7. Public holidays

The company observes 12 paid public holidays each year. The Kochi and Mumbai offices publish separate holiday calendars every December because regional holidays differ. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off, to be taken within 60 days.

## 8. Applying for leave

Annual leave must be applied for through the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance and approved by the reporting manager. Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days also needs approval from the department head. Emergency leave may be applied for retrospectively within 3 working days of returning to work.

## 9. Leave without pay

After all paid annual leave is used, employees may request up to 30 days of leave without pay in a calendar year. Requests need approval from the department head and HR. Leave without pay longer than 30 days is treated as a career break and requires approval from the Managing Director.
