# Code of Conduct and Grievance Procedure

Konkan Marine Services Pvt. Ltd. (fictional company). Synthetic document for demonstration. Version 4.1, effective 1 June 2026.

## 1. Purpose

This code sets out the standards of behaviour expected of all shore staff, and how employees can raise concerns. Breaches of the code may lead to disciplinary action under section 9. The code applies in the office, at client sites, on board vessels during visits, and at company events.

## 2. Working hours and attendance

Standard working hours are 9:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Employees must record attendance in the HR portal every working day. Arriving more than 30 minutes late three times in a month counts as half a day of annual leave.

## 3. Remote work

Employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval, once they have completed 6 months of service. Remote work is not permitted during the first and last week of each financial quarter for finance staff. Employees working remotely must be reachable on the company's chat system during core hours of 10:30 to 16:30.

## 4. Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts worth up to INR 3,000 from a business partner, no more than once per calendar year from the same partner. Any gift worth more must be declared to the Compliance Officer within 7 days and may need to be returned. Cash, gift cards and vouchers may never be accepted, whatever their value.

## 5. Conflicts of interest

Employees must declare any personal financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor, and any close relative working for one, using the annual conflict-of-interest form. New conflicts must be declared within 30 days of arising. Employees may not take part in a purchasing decision involving a company in which they have a declared interest.

## 6. Confidential information

Commercial terms, crew personal data, vessel performance data and client correspondence are confidential. Confidential information may only be shared with people who need it for their work. Employees must not forward company documents to personal email accounts. Confidentiality obligations continue for 2 years after employment ends.

## 7. Harassment and the Internal Committee

The company does not tolerate harassment, including sexual harassment. Complaints of sexual harassment can be made to the Internal Committee in writing within 3 months of the incident, a period the committee may extend by another 3 months for valid reasons. The committee completes its inquiry within 90 days. Retaliation against anyone who raises a complaint is itself a disciplinary offence.

## 8. Grievance procedure

An employee with a work-related grievance should first raise it with their reporting manager, who must respond within 7 working days. If the grievance is not resolved, the employee may escalate it in writing to the Head of HR, who must hold a meeting within 10 working days. The final appeal lies with the Managing Director, whose decision is final.

## 9. Disciplinary action

Depending on seriousness, breaches of this code lead to a verbal warning, a written warning, suspension, or dismissal. A written warning stays on the employee's file for 12 months. Serious misconduct, such as fraud, violence, or disclosure of confidential information, may lead to dismissal without prior warning.
