# Notices to Port Users 2026 (Kochi and Mumbai)

Synthetic document for demonstration. These notices are invented and must not be used for real navigation or port operations.

## 1. Pre-arrival notification

The vessel's agent must submit the pre-arrival notification at least 72 hours before the estimated time of arrival, or on departure from the last port if the voyage is shorter than 72 hours. The notification includes the crew list, cargo manifest, dangerous goods declaration, ISPS security information and the last 10 ports of call. Berth allocation is confirmed only after the notification is accepted.

## 2. Pilot boarding

At Kochi the pilot boards at the outer anchorage, 4 nautical miles west of the fairway buoy. At Mumbai the pilot boards 2 nautical miles south of the Prongs Reef light. Vessels must request a pilot at least 6 hours in advance through the port control VHF channel 12 and confirm 2 hours before arrival at the boarding point.

## 3. Monsoon restrictions

From 1 June to 30 September, the Kochi approach channel is restricted to vessels with a draft of no more than 12.5 metres, regardless of berth depth. Pilotage at both ports is suspended when the sustained wind speed exceeds 30 knots or the significant wave height exceeds 3.5 metres at the pilot boarding point.

## 4. Night navigation

Vessels longer than 250 metres may enter or leave the Kochi channel only between 06:00 and 18:00. At Mumbai, liquid bulk tankers carrying crude oil may berth at Jawahar Dweep at night only with the Harbour Master's written permission, requested at least 12 hours in advance.

## 5. Anchorage

Vessels waiting for a berth must anchor in the designated outer anchorage. Anchorage is free for the first 72 hours, after which it is charged at USD 0.02 per GT per day. Vessels carrying dangerous goods anchor in the separate dangerous goods anchorage, at least 1 nautical mile from other vessels.

## 6. Bunkering

Bunkering at berth is permitted only for vessels at the oil and LNG terminals and needs a bunkering permit from the Harbour Master, requested 24 hours in advance. Bunkering at anchorage is allowed in daylight hours only and is suspended when the wind exceeds 20 knots.

## 7. Waste reception

Garbage and oily waste may be discharged only to port-authorised reception facilities. The waste notification form must be submitted 24 hours before arrival. Each vessel is charged a fixed waste fee of USD 350 per call, whether or not waste is delivered, which covers up to 5 cubic metres of garbage.

## 8. Security

Both ports operate at ISPS security level 1 unless announced otherwise. Visitors to a vessel need a port entry pass, applied for by the agent at least 24 hours in advance. Crew shore leave requires a valid shore pass issued by immigration authorities on arrival.
