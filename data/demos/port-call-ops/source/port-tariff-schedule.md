# Port Tariff Schedule 2026 (Kochi and Mumbai)

Synthetic document for demonstration. The figures below are invented and are not the tariffs of any real port authority. Valid from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

## 1. Applicability

This schedule applies to all foreign-going and coastal vessels calling at berths listed in the berth register for Kochi and Mumbai. Charges are levied per call and are quoted in US dollars for foreign-going vessels. Coastal vessels pay 60 percent of the foreign-going rate for port dues and pilotage.

## 2. Port dues

Port dues are charged at USD 0.45 per gross tonne (GT) per call for foreign-going vessels. Vessels calling only for bunkering, crew change or stores, and staying less than 24 hours, pay 50 percent of port dues. Vessels that call more than 12 times in a financial year receive a 15 percent rebate on port dues from the 13th call onwards.

## 3. Pilotage

Pilotage is compulsory for all vessels above 500 GT. The pilotage fee is USD 0.18 per GT for each inward or outward movement, subject to a minimum of USD 1,200 per movement. Each shifting between berths counts as a separate movement. Pilot detention caused by the vessel is charged at USD 250 per hour or part hour.

## 4. Towage

Tug requirements depend on vessel length. Vessels up to 150 m length overall need 1 tug, vessels from 150 m to 250 m need 2 tugs, and vessels longer than 250 m need 3 tugs for berthing and unberthing. Each tug is charged at USD 1,800 per operation.

## 5. Berth hire

Berth hire is charged per day at the daily rate shown for each berth in the berth register. Part days are rounded up to the next half day. Berth hire starts when the first mooring line is made fast and ends when the last line is cast off. Idle time caused by port equipment breakdown is not charged.

## 6. Overtime and holidays

Pilotage and towage performed between 22:00 and 06:00, or on a public holiday, attract a surcharge of 25 percent. Cargo-handling overtime is billed separately by the terminal operator and is not part of this schedule.

## 7. Green vessel rebate

Vessels holding a valid Environmental Ship Index score of 30 or more receive a 10 percent rebate on port dues. Vessels using shore power while at berth receive an additional 5 percent rebate on berth hire. Rebates are applied on the final invoice and are not cumulative beyond 20 percent in total.

## 8. Payment terms

The vessel's agent must deposit 100 percent of the estimated port charges before berthing. The final invoice is issued within 5 working days of departure, and any balance is payable within 15 days. Late payments attract interest of 1.5 percent per month.
