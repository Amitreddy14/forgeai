# Travel and Expenses Policy

## 1. Booking
All business travel must be booked through the travel desk at least 7 days in advance.
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; business class needs director
approval.

## 2. Daily allowance
The daily meal allowance for domestic travel is INR 1,500. For international travel the
allowance is USD 60 per day. Receipts are required for any single expense above INR 2,000.

## 3. Hotels
Hotel stays are capped at INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night
elsewhere. Stays above the cap need prior approval from the travel desk.

## 4. Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning from travel. Late claims
are paid only with finance approval.
