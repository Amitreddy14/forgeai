# Leave Policy (Shore Staff)

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains leave entitlements for shore-based staff of the company. It does
not apply to seafarers, whose leave is set by their employment agreements.

## 2. Annual leave
Shore staff earn 24 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at two days
per completed month of service. Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next
year with manager approval; any other unused days lapse on 31 December.

## 3. Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

## 4. Parental leave
Birth mothers receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Other parents receive 15 working
days of paid parental leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## 5. Applying for leave
Apply through the HR portal at least 10 working days before planned leave. Emergency
leave may be applied for retrospectively within 3 working days of returning.
